Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?

No. You can start your 14-day free trial and explore every Pro feature without entering payment details.

Which tools does Flowbase connect to?

Flowbase integrates with Google Sheets, HubSpot, Salesforce, Stripe, Notion, Slack, and 40+ other tools ops teams already use.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts — cancel from your billing settings in one click and you won't be charged again.

What happens if I hit my plan's report limit?

We'll notify you before you hit the limit. You can upgrade instantly, and nothing you've already built is ever deleted or paused.

Is my client data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Flowbase never sells or shares customer data, and you can request full deletion at any time.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Yes — annual plans save 20% compared to monthly billing, applied automatically at checkout.

Can my whole team use one account?

Pro and Business plans support multiple seats with shared templates, so anyone on the team can build or edit a report.

What if I need a custom report format?

The Business plan includes custom template design — our team will build your exact client format during onboarding.
