Pricing questions, answered

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts on Starter or Pro — cancel from billing settings in one click and you won't be charged again.

What happens if I hit my plan's limits?

We'll email you before you hit a limit. You can upgrade instantly and nothing you've built is paused or deleted while you decide.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Yes — switch the toggle above. Annual billing saves 20% compared to paying monthly, applied automatically at checkout.

Is Flowbase good for freelancers, or only teams?

Both. Starter is built for solo operators; Pro and Business add seats, shared templates, and permissions as your team grows.

What counts as a "seat"?

A seat is one team member with login access to build or edit reports. Clients who only receive reports don't count against your seat limit.

How does security and compliance work for enterprise?

Business plan customers get SSO/SAML, audit logs, and a signed DPA. We're SOC 2 Type II audited annually — reach out to sales for our report.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes, Pro and Business both include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required to start.

What if I need something Business doesn't cover?

Talk to sales — we support custom contracts, invoicing terms, and bespoke integrations for larger accounts.
